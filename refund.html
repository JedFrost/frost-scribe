Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Subscription pricing

Frost Scribe Pro is currently offered at ₹199 per month or ₹1,999 per year, unless a different price is clearly shown at checkout.

2. Cancellation

You may cancel a Pro subscription using the available Frost Scribe billing controls. Cancellation stops future renewal and normally takes effect at the end of the current paid billing cycle. Pro access generally remains available through the paid period.

3. Refund eligibility

Subscription charges are generally non-refundable once a billing period has started, except where required by applicable law or where Frost Scribe confirms a duplicate charge, erroneous charge, unauthorized transaction, or qualifying service failure after review.

4. Refund request process

Send refund requests to jedfrostofficial@gmail.com with the relevant payment reference and Discord server information. Do not send full card numbers, PINs, passwords or other banking credentials.

5. Refund timeline

If a refund is approved, Frost Scribe will normally initiate it within 5–7 business days. After initiation, the time for the amount to appear in your account depends on Razorpay, your bank, card issuer or payment method.

6. Failed or reversed payments

If a payment fails, is reversed or becomes invalid, Pro access may be suspended or returned to Free until payment is successfully restored.

7. Contact

Email: jedfrostofficial@gmail.com
