Service Delivery

Service Delivery / Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Digital service

Frost Scribe is a digital Discord bot and software service. No physical goods are sold or shipped.

2. Free service delivery

Free features are delivered digitally when the Frost Scribe bot is added to a supported Discord server and configured by an authorized server administrator.

3. Pro service delivery

After a successful Pro subscription payment and confirmation from the payment provider, Pro access is intended to be activated automatically for the associated Discord server. Activation normally occurs shortly after successful payment confirmation.

4. Delivery issues

If payment succeeds but Pro access does not activate, contact jedfrostofficial@gmail.com with the payment reference and Discord server information. Support will investigate the entitlement and payment status.

5. No shipping charges

Because Frost Scribe is delivered digitally, there are no physical shipping charges, courier fees or delivery addresses required.

6. Geographic availability

Frost Scribe is delivered online through Discord and may be used where Discord and the relevant payment services are available, subject to applicable law and service-provider restrictions.

Email: jedfrostofficial@gmail.com
