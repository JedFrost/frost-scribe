Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Frost Scribe processes information when the Discord bot is used for meeting scheduling, attendance tracking, voice recording, subscription management and AI-powered meeting notes.

1. Information we process

  • Discord server, channel and user identifiers needed to operate bot features.
  • Meeting configuration, scheduling data and attendance records.
  • Voice audio captured only when an authorized user starts a recording.
  • Transcripts and AI-generated summaries for eligible Pro sessions.
  • Subscription status and payment-related references needed to manage Pro access. Payment card or bank credentials are handled by Razorpay and are not stored by Frost Scribe.

2. How we use information

Information is used to provide requested Discord bot functionality, generate attendance exports, process recordings, create Pro transcription/summaries, manage subscriptions, prevent abuse, diagnose service problems and improve reliability.

3. Recording files

Recording files are intended to be temporary processing files. They are created while a recording is active, used to deliver recording outputs and, for Pro, to generate transcription and summaries. Frost Scribe is designed to remove these temporary files from its runtime storage after processing and delivery.

4. Third-party services

Frost Scribe operates through Discord and uses third-party infrastructure, AI and payment services. Discord's own terms and privacy practices apply to Discord accounts and messages. Razorpay processes subscription payments and its own privacy terms apply to payment information.

5. Recording consent

Meeting organizers and server administrators are responsible for providing appropriate notice and obtaining any consent required before recording participants.

6. Retention

Configuration and subscription records may be retained while needed to provide the service or meet legitimate operational, accounting or legal requirements. Temporary recording files are not intended for long-term storage by Frost Scribe.
